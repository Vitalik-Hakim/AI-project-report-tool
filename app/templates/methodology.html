Methodology and System Architecture

Detailed technical description of our 5-layer hybrid analysis engine — combining 6 classical deterministic financial screening modules, an independently trained supervised ML distress classifier, a composite confidence comparison layer, and a strictly constrained LLM narrative synthesis.

1. Supervised ML Distress Classifier

Calibrated Random Forest Ensemble

Our predictive distress model is a Calibrated Random Forest Classifier trained on historical corporate filing outcomes. The model operates with class-weighted balancing to account for the empirical ~3.9% bankruptcy rate in public filings, outputting true posterior probabilities of distress ($P(\text{distress})$).

Model Specifications & Design Rules:

  • Independent Outcome Labels: Strictly trained on real historical corporate outcomes — never trained on Altman or Piotroski scores.
  • Balanced Weighting: Addresses severe class imbalance via balanced sample weights rather than synthetic resampling.
  • Probability Calibration: Calibrated via sigmoid/isotonic regression to ensure probabilistic outputs are well-calibrated confidence estimates.
2. Comprehensive Rule-Based Fundamental Suite (6 Pillars)

1. DuPont 3-Step Analysis (ROE Decomposition)

Dissects Return on Equity into three operational drivers to identify whether returns stem from genuine operational margins, efficient asset utilization, or financial engineering/leverage:

ROE = Net Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier
Net Profit Margin (Net Income / Revenue) — Operating profitability and pricing power.
Asset Turnover (Revenue / Total Assets) — Capital efficiency in revenue generation.
Equity Multiplier (Total Assets / Total Equity) — Financial leverage multiplier.

2. Cash Quality & Conversion Engine

Measures whether accounting net income is backed by cash receipts, evaluates Free Cash Flow after capital expenditures, and monitors conversion health:

Cash Conversion Ratio (CCR)

CCR = Operating Cash Flow / Net Income

> 1.0 indicates earnings are fully backed by operating cash receipts.

Free Cash Flow Conversion

FCF = OCF - CapEx | FCF Conversion = FCF / NI

≥ 80% Strong · 50-80% Moderate · < 50% Watch/Weak

3. ROIC vs. WACC (Economic Value Added)

Assesses whether the business generates returns exceeding its opportunity cost of capital:

Economic Spread = ROIC - WACC
ROIC = NOPAT / Operating Invested Capital (Total Assets - Cash - Current Liabilities).
WACC = (We × Ke) + (Wd × Kd × (1 - t)), where Ke is estimated via CAPM (Rf + β × MRP).
Value Creation: Spread > 0 indicates value creation; Spread < 0 indicates value destruction.

4. Piotroski F-Score (9-Point Fundamental Checklist)

9-point binary checklist evaluating year-over-year fundamental momentum across Profitability, Leverage/Liquidity, and Operating Efficiency:

Profitability (4 pts)

  • Positive Net Income
  • Positive Operating Cash Flow
  • Improving ROA
  • Operating Cash Flow > Net Income

Leverage / Liquidity (3 pts)

  • Lower Long-Term Debt Ratio
  • Improving Current Ratio
  • No New Shares Issued

Efficiency (2 pts)

  • Improving Gross Margin
  • Improving Asset Turnover

5. Altman Z-Score (Solvency Screening)

Linear combination evaluating solvency and liquidity:

Z = 1.2 · X₁ + 1.4 · X₂ + 3.3 · X₃ + 0.6 · X₄ + 1.0 · X₅

Distress Zone

< 1.81

Grey Zone

1.81 – 2.99

Safe Zone

> 2.99

6. Beneish M-Score (Earnings Quality & Manipulation Screening)

8-variable forensic accounting screening model assessing aggressive accruals or revenue recognition risks.

Risk Classification Threshold: M-Score > -1.78 indicates potential manipulation risk
3. Agreement & Divergence Comparison Layer

Balanced Confidence and Neutral Divergence

Rule-based scores are synthesized into a balanced composite confidence:

Confidence = 20% (Altman) + 20% (Piotroski) + 15% (DuPont) + 15% (Cash Quality) + 15% (ROIC/WACC) + 15% (Beneish)

Agreement Condition: If |Rule-Based Confidence - Model Confidence| ≤ 15 points, the signals independently converge.

Divergence Condition: If the gap exceeds 15 points, the system highlights the category driver (leverage, cash conversion, or margin decay) with strictly neutral framing — highlighting divergence as an area for deeper diligence, without assuming either model is right or wrong.

Mistral AI Synthesis Brief

The final text summary is compiled using Mistral Large under strict prompt constraints: no metric recalculation, no hallucinated external facts, and strictly neutral divergence framing.
